NAU undergraduate capstone · UGRADS

Shot difficulty and scoring across the NBA and NHL

How much of a player's shooting efficiency reflects the difficulty of the shots they take? This capstone uses matched 2014–2024 NBA and NHL shot data to study expected scoring, shot difficulty, and player performance.

Open full-resolution graph to zoom

My work: Developed data preparation and validation workflows, shot-quality models, a Shot Difficulty Index, and visual comparisons of player performance. The team presented the capstone at NAU UGRADS.
