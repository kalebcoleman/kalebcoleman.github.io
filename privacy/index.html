Privacy

Builds and preferences stay in your browser unless you choose to create a free cloud account. Screenshots and face photos are processed on-device.

Local data

Theme, language, cap-breaker level, saved builds and other preferences are stored in this browser. If you never create an account, no build data is sent to a server.

Optional free cloud account

Creating an account is optional and free. Supabase stores your email address, build names, build codes and timestamps. It does not store screenshots, face photos, payment details or analytics. The Supabase client library is loaded from jsDelivr; Supabase handles authentication and the database. No other third-party trackers or advertising scripts are used.

Screenshots and face photos

Builder screenshots are read in this browser with the recovered layout reader and are never uploaded. Facescan photos are processed in the browser; the Facescan tool may fetch public Wikimedia/Openverse images when you search for a name, but your own photos stay on your device.

Deleting data

Sign in, open Cloud builds in the builder and choose Delete account. That deletes the auth user and, by cascade, every cloud build saved to it. Local browser data remains until you clear this site's storage in your browser settings. See the Delete account page for details.

Contact

This is an unofficial fan project. For a data request or a bug, use the repository issue tracker linked from the project page. Do not send account passwords.
