Tips for photos that scan well
  • Even light, with no strong colour from screens or stage lights.
  • A neutral face, mouth closed, no glasses.
  • Have someone take them from about 5 feet back, zoomed in a little: arm's-length selfies stretch the nose.
  • The photo that faces the camera most becomes the front one. Tap another photo, or use Reorder, to change it.
