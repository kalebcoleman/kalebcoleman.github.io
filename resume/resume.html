twinsmith: UR5e Digital Twin from Wrist-Camera Data (Isaac Sim) [code] [benchmark]Oct 2026 – Present
  • Match real UR5e wrist-camera recordings to part poses and rebuild the scene in Isaac Sim (Newton). On one still five-part clip, all five parts matched and the rebuild settled by at most 0.1 mm; stacked and leaning layouts are only partly solved, and that clip has no independent ground truth.
