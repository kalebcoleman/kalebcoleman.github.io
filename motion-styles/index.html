Motion styles

Every motion style a MyPLAYER of your height can wear, with the recovered height range, Speed and Agility asks, and how fast the reference test builds jogged and sprinted in each one.

The short version

  1. Your height picks the list Each style is for a range of MyPLAYER heights and can ask for a Speed and an Agility rating. In the recovered table, that is all that locks a style.
  2. Off the ball A motion style moves you when you run without the ball, on offense or on defense. With the ball, your dribble style moves you instead.
  3. Recommended At each height, the recommended style has the fastest offense sprint and defense sprint together. Ties go to the higher top speed.

Three height groups

The table was measured on a 6'2", a 6'6" and a 7'0" build. Every style's height range starts and ends on one of these band edges, so each height uses its band's build times.

    How it works

    • Three test builds. Every style was timed on a 6'2", a 6'6" and a 7'0" build. The height ranges start and end on the 5'9" to 6'4", 6'5" to 6'9" and 6'10" to 7'4" band edges, so each height shows the times of its band's build.
    • Four runs. Offense jog, offense sprint, defense jog and defense sprint, each 47 ft of half court in a real possession: its time and its top speed.
    • Not timed yet. Seven styles a 5'9" to 6'4" MyPLAYER can wear were only ever timed on the 6'6" build. At those heights they show no times and sort last. The catalog never borrows times from another build.
    • Recommended. The fastest combined offense and defense sprint time at the selected height, with ties going to the higher combined top speed. Search and rating filters do not change the recommendation.

    What it doesn't do

    • Not your dribble speed. Nothing the dribbling player's speed comes from reads the motion style: Speed With Ball sets it.
    • Close times are a tie. A run's 47 ft time moves about 0.05 s from one run to the next, and a second pass put the styles in a different order, so styles a few hundredths apart are even. Top speed repeats better than time.
    • Recovered, not re-run. The height gates and timing runs are the reference site's recovered data. This rebuild does not claim to have re-timed the game.

    How we know

    The height ranges and Speed/Agility asks come from the game's own motion style table. The times are the original Goated Build Lab in-game runs, with the style written straight into the player before each run. The gates were read on Oct 16, 2026 and the times were run on Oct 15, 2026; the reference page was updated Oct 29, 2026.
